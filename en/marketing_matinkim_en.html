POP-UP STORE - Matin Kim, Umeda Hankyu Department

Following a successful first pop-up in Tokyo, the brand continued its momentum by holding a second pop-up in Osaka—Japan’s second-largest city, known for its Korean-friendly culture.
The pop-up was hosted at Hankyu Department Store, one of the most influential department stores in Osaka, further reinforcing the brand’s presence.
Branding Point

To emphasize brand identity, the space was designed with a white base and accented with metallic decorations to create a refined atmosphere.
A photo zone was installed outside the store, featuring a large brand monument and mannequins, showcasing the brand’s experimental spirit.

Given the high foot traffic and long queues compared to other brands, we ensured a spacious layout for smooth visitor flow and designed the store to provide clear visibility of the items from outside.
