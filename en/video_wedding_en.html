WEDDING CELEBRATION VIDEOS - Nakamura's Wedding

I planned and produced two wedding celebration videos.
One is an emotional, music-video style film featuring only the bride and groom.
The other is an interview-style video centered on both mothers, sharing memories and heartfelt messages.
Both were screened on the wedding day.

1st VIDEO - Nakamura's Wedding Celebration

The first video features only the bride and groom.
It was filmed in Gifu(氣候), the groom’s hometown and where the couple will begin their newlywed life.
The video captures their emotions—happiness and anticipation—through visuals and music alone, without dialogue, using a music video-style approach.
Warm color grading was applied throughout to create a consistent and intimate mood.

2nd VIDEO - Mothers’ Voice

A documentary-style interview with both mothers sharing memories and heartfelt messages.
I asked about the happiest moment they had shared with their son or daughter.
Their natural responses were woven into a narrative, followed by the couple sharing their hopes and messages.
The video emphasizes authenticity—capturing facial expressions, pauses, and laughter—leading gracefully into the wedding ceremony.
