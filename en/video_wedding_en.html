WEDDING CELEBRATION VIDEOS - Nakamura's Wedding

I planned and produced two wedding celebration videos.
One is a mood-driven visual piece featuring only the bride and groom, and the other is an interview-based documentary centered on both mothers’ memories and blessings.

Both videos were screened during the wedding ceremony.

1st VIDEO - Nakamura's Wedding Celebration

This video features only the bride and groom.
Filmed in Gifu, the groom’s hometown and the couple’s future home, it captures the joy and anticipation of their new life together.
The film uses music and visuals alone—without narration—to express emotion, much like a music video.
Warm tones were applied during color grading to unify the atmosphere.

2nd VIDEO - Mothers’ Voice

The second video is structured like a short documentary. It begins with personal memories from each mother and leads into heartfelt blessings for the couple’s future. Through the question “What has been your happiest moment with your child?”, we captured sincere and natural reflections.

Careful editing ensured a natural flow by aligning their facial expressions, pauses, and laughter with question progression.
In the latter part of the video, the bride and groom appear to share their hopes for married life and express gratitude to their guests— creating a seamless transition into the wedding ceremony.
The direction focused on capturing authenticity, emphasizing emotional nuance over staged moments.
