BRAND CAMPAIGN VIDEO - Design Your History, Moogoong

I planned and produced a campaign video for the virtual brand "Moogoong."
The film connects the fading of raw denim—reflecting daily wear— and the permanence of tattoos—marking meaning on denim.
Brand Story

Moogoong, inspired by the Korean word "Mugungmujin" (무궁무진, infinite possibilities),
is a denim brand focused on both expansion and change.
It highlights the craftsmanship of selvage denim and the aesthetic that evolves with wear.
It reinterprets traditional denim to be accessible to modern consumers.

Video Concept

The protagonist is tattoo artist Minyoung.
The video begins with his reflecting on his career.

He considers how tattoos preserve memories, thoughts, and meaning.
He recalls conversations with clients and mentions denim stains from tattoo ink.
These permanent marks resonate with the fading of raw denim—another form of lived memory.

Both tattoos and jeans carry personal meaning.
Moogoong’s message: accumulated traces reveal identity.
The campaign visually explores this parallel to reinforce brand identity.
