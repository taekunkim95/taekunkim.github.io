LOGO DESIGN - Garam Project, VJ Studio

I worked on the logo design for the VJ studio GARAM PROJECT.
The identity was applied across various online and offline environments, including business cards, uniforms, and screens.
Brand Logo

Centered on the text “GARAM PROJECT,” graphic elements were arranged to deliver a strong and creative visual impression.
The logo reflects the studio’s philosophy of pursuing progressive and experimental projects.

Design Concept

Drawing on the unique qualities of a VJ studio, the design visualizes “flow” as seen in moving images, as well as the energy of youth and dynamism.

Color

BLUE: Conveys a sense of trust, professionalism, and technical precision.
BLACK: Creates strong contrast with blue, enhancing both readability and visual impact.

Typography &  Pattern

A bold, linear sans-serif typeface was used to convey modernity and strong visual presence.
Slants were added to the top and bottom of the typography to express motion.
The type was designed to break beyond the dotted circular frame, symbolizing freedom beyond boundaries.

A blue dot pattern was used to evoke a digital and lively feel. It symbolizes experimentation, creativity, and youth, while also visually anchoring the freely styled typography.
The combination of both elements creates a visually dynamic yet structured image, expressing both dimensionality and freedom within a 2D space.
