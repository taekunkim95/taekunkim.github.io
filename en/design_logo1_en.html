LOGO DESIGN - Garam Project, VJ Studio

We developed a logo identity project for “GARAM PROJECT,” a VJ studio known for its experimental and creative direction.
The branding was applied across both online and offline mediums including business cards, uniforms, and screens, maintaining a cohesive identity throughout all touchpoints.
Brand Logo

The logo centers around the name “GARAM PROJECT” with impactful graphic elements that reflect the studio’s bold and forward-thinking spirit.
The identity captures the studio’s philosophy of experimentation and progressive vision.

Design Concept

The brand reflects the VJ studio's visual DNA—flow, dynamism and youth—expressed through dynamic shapes and graphic movement.

Color

BLUE : communicates trust, professionalism, and a technical edge.
BLACK : offers strong contrast and delivers a bold visual impact while ensuring readability.

Typography &  Pattern

We chose a bold, modern sans-serif typeface to express confidence and a contemporary sensibility.
The top and bottom of the typography were angled to add visual dynamism, while breaking out of the dotted circular frame suggests freedom and flexibility.

Blue dotted patterns represent a digital feel, embodying youth, experimentation, and creativity.
Combined with dynamic typography, the design conveys a 2D-to-3D dimensional shift, expressing energy and playfulness.
