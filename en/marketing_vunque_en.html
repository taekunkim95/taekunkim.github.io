LAUNCHING PARTY - vunque, Omotesando

Participated in the Japan launch party for the Korean bag brand vunque.
This was the brand’s first step into the Japanese market, aimed at building brand awareness and establishing a network among local industry professionals.
The event invited celebrities, media, and fashion insiders from Korea and Japan—including Oh My Girl’s YooA and actor Ji-hoon Lee.
As the brand’s first official event in Japan, it focused on building a premium brand image and laying the foundation for future marketing efforts.
Branding Point

The 120m² venue was divided into zones for reception, catering, interviews, and party & exhibition areas to increase visitor engagement and immersion in the brand.
To reflect the importance of this launch, the space was built around the brand's logo and signature design elements.
As a mid-to-high-end bag brand, the spatial concept emphasized a premium atmosphere.
