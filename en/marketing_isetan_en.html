MULTI BRAND POP-UP - X-mas Edge, Shinjuku Isetan

X-mas Edge was a multi-brand Christmas-themed pop-up store, featuring a unique and sophisticated silver-toned concept that differentiated it from traditional Christmas atmospheres.
Four popular Korean brands (vunque / STAND OIL / sienne / NUMBERING) participated in a joint pop-up at Shinjuku Isetan, one of the most prestigious department stores in Japan.

This project was conducted by MXN JAPAN.
I participated in space design, vendor coordination, client communication, and event planning.
Branding Point

Considering the holiday season, we set the theme as “Silver Christmas” and applied it throughout the store's interior.
Crinkled silver materials were installed on the central wall to reinforce the silver motif and create visual unity.
A cart and gift boxes were placed at the main monument zone to deliver a direct and indirect image of gifting—for oneself or others.
To maintain each brand’s identity while unifying the overall space, fixtures were selected in achromatic tones such as white-silver and black-black. Through this, we aimed to highlight each brand's identity while delivering a cohesive and seasonal store experience.
