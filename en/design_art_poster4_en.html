ART POSTER SERIES 2 - Masion Margiela Paris

This is a personal art poster project inspired by the identity and visual elements of a fashion brand.
Focusing on strong visual impact, I reinterpreted the brand's imagery using logos, various typefaces,
and symbolic elements in a single composition.
The works were designed with both content context and physical display environments in mind.
Project Overview
Maison Margiela is a French fashion house known for its deconstructivist approach and its defiance of traditional fashion norms.
Drawing on the brand’s visual identity, I incorporated geometric shapes
and abstract elements into a free-form layout that reflects the spirit of deconstruction.
